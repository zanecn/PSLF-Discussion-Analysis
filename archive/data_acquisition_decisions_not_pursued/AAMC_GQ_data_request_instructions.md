# AAMC Graduation Questionnaire (GQ) Data Request Instructions

The AAMC GQ has annual data on PSLF intent, specialty intent, and debt levels for graduating MD students since at least 2008. **Specialty intent × PSLF intent crosstabs require a custom data request** (not in the public Summary Report).

## Step 1: Determine what you need

For the substantive paper, the most useful variables are:
- **PSLF intent** (have you / do you plan to participate in PSLF?) — annual since ~2008
- **Specialty intent** (Q on intended specialty)
- **Debt levels** (intent question + debt amount question)
- **NHSC/military/state forgiveness intent** (parallel options)

The crosstabs that would directly support our cohort-heterogeneity story:
- PSLF intent × specialty (annual 2010-2024)
- PSLF intent × debt level (annual 2010-2024)
- PSLF intent × demographic stratifiers (race, gender, parental income)

## Step 2: Request via the AAMC Data Request portal

**URL**: https://www.aamc.org/request-aamc-data
**Email**: gq@aamc.org (GQ-specific) or datarequest@aamc.org (general)

**Request form needs**:
- Description of your project (computational text-analysis of PSLF discourse)
- Variables needed (PSLF intent, specialty, debt level — annual since 2010)
- Whether you want microdata (de-identified individual responses) or aggregate tables
- IRB status (you'll likely need IRB approval for sensitive variables)
- Authorship plan
- Funding source

## Step 3: Expect 6-12 weeks for DUA approval

The AAMC reviews case-by-case. For sensitive variables (debt, career, PSLF intent), you must demonstrate:
- IRB approval at your institution (or exempt determination)
- A research plan with specific hypotheses
- Data security plan

If you're at an LCME-member medical school, your institution already has access to its OWN GQ data, but cross-institutional analysis still requires the DUA.

## Step 4: Cost

No flat fee, but custom data extracts have per-request charges:
- Aggregate tables: typically $500–1,500
- Microdata files: typically $1,500–5,000+
- Linked AAMC databases (e.g., GQ × Faculty Roster): higher

You'll get a fee quote BEFORE accepting.

## Step 5: What to do with the data when it arrives

Match against our discourse data on three axes:
1. **Year-over-year PSLF intent rate** vs SDN PSLF discussion volume
2. **Specialty × PSLF intent crosstab** vs our SDN per-specialty discussion
3. **Debt level × PSLF intent** vs our discourse rejection-reason patterns

This would give the substantive paper a direct survey-data triangulation that strengthens external validity dramatically.

## Public-data fallback (use while waiting for DUA)

While the DUA is pending, you can use the public **All Schools Summary Report** (Excel):
- 2024: https://www.aamc.org/media/78536/download
- Earlier years available in AAMC GQ archive: https://www.aamc.org/data-reports/students-residents/report/graduation-questionnaire-gq

These give aggregate PSLF intent percentages (not crosstabs) but are sufficient for year-over-year trend visualization.

## Recommended action

1. **Apply for DUA NOW** (no cost; ~3 month process)
2. **Use public summary tables for the paper draft**
3. **If DUA approves before submission**, add the crosstab analysis as a supplementary results section
4. **If DUA approves after submission**, plan it as a v2 paper or follow-up

The DUA process is slow but the data is genuinely high-value for the workforce-relevance claim. Start the application now if you intend to pursue the workforce-implications angle.
