MOTIVATION
----------
The Round-7 policy-implications audit flagged the "Final Trump Rule = -13.5 pp
rejecting-rate change" as a potential floor-effect artifact, noting that two
cells (SDN-Medical and Finance) hit exactly 0.0% rejecting post-rule. This
investigation tests whether the apparent rejecting-rate collapse is:
  (a) a genuine within-person stance change (relief),
  (b) a topic-mix shift mechanically diluting rejecting (post-rule discourse
      shifted heavily to policy_uncertainty, which has 58% unknown stance),
  (c) a composition shift (different authors posting before vs after), or
  (d) a Claude-prompt artifact (stance classifier behaving differently on
      post-rule vocabulary).

This is a CRITICAL finding. The -43.9 pp rejecting drop is ENTIRELY a

================================================================================
INTERPRETATION & POLICY IMPLICATIONS
================================================================================

The pre/post stance/intention shifts in this corpus reflect TWO distinct
phenomena that the analysis cannot separately identify:

  1. WITHIN-PERSON CHANGE: Same poster updates their stance after the event.
  2. COMPOSITION CHANGE: Different posters show up to discuss the event
     (selection by topic relevance, by mood-driven engagement, by departure).

Both are POLICY-RELEVANT but mean very different things:

  - "Borrowers changed their minds about pursuing PSLF" requires within-person
    evidence. We cannot establish this without per-author longitudinal panels.
  - "The discussant pool pivoted around the event" is what most of the +/-
    pp shifts in this analysis actually capture.

For the Final Trump Rule -13.5 pp aggregate (and -43.9 pp SDN-Medical) finding,
the COMPOSITION-only interpretation is the safer claim:
  "After the Final Trump PSLF Rule, the SDN-Medical discussion was populated
  by entirely different posters with substantially different stance
  distributions. We cannot distinguish whether existing posters with
  rejecting intent stopped posting (cohort exit) or new posters with
  considering/pursuing intent arrived (cohort entry)."

For the Trump PSLF EO +2.5 pp NS finding (highest overlap at 41.8%), the
within-person component is largest, but still <50% — composition turnover
remains substantial.

REPHRASED HEADLINE FINDINGS (incorporating audit + composition concerns):

  - Final Trump Rule "rejecting drop": composition-shift artifact.
    The 18 pre-rule rejecting authors did not return; 14 new authors
    populated post-window discourse with different priors.
  - SAVE Forbearance "rejecting drop": 34% within-person, 66% composition.
  - Trump PSLF EO "no shift": 42% within-person continuity supports the
    "no detectable change" reading; the modest +2.5 pp may even reflect
    composition turnover toward less-committed posters.
  - IDR Account Adjustment "+10 pp rejecting": 36% within-person; the
    within-topic + topic-mix corroboration (financial_planning +49 pp)
    makes this the most defensible behavioral finding even after
    composition concerns.
  - Limited PSLF Waiver "+6 pp rejecting": 18% within-person — the LOWEST.
    Almost entirely composition turnover. The "good-news anxiety"
    mechanism is even less defensible than the audit suggested.

================================================================================
WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE PAPER
================================================================================

  1. ALL pre/post stance shifts must be reframed as "discussant-pool stance
     composition shifts" rather than "borrower stance changes."

  2. Per-author longitudinal analysis is REQUIRED for any claim about
     within-person stance dynamics. With ~30-40% overlap across most events,
     this is partially feasible on the existing corpus.

  3. The IDR Account Adjustment finding is the most robust behavioral signal
     because the topic-shift corroboration (+49 pp financial_planning) holds
     even with composition turnover — people newly arriving to discuss IDR
     adjustment are more likely to be doing detailed planning than to be
     stating rejecting.

  4. The Final Trump PSLF Rule finding should be substantively REVISED in
     CLAUDE.md from "relief from less-catastrophic rule" to "composition
     turnover with rejecting-stance posters absent from post-rule discourse."

  5. The substantive paper's "stance dynamics" angle is weakened by this
     finding. The strongest remaining substantive claims are:
     (a) topic-mix shifts around events (composition-immune; topic of post
         depends on event, not on poster identity)
     (b) sentiment-stance decoupling (cross-sectional, not longitudinal)
     (c) construct-mismatch finding (methodological)
