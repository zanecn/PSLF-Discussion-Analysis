CAVEAT (Round-7 re-audit): HC3 corrects heteroskedasticity but NOT
autocorrelation. Posts within a 60-90d event window are topically
clustered (one news event spawns a cascade), so HC3 SEs are still
anti-conservative. The 30-100x gap between HC3 p and bootstrap p above
is the autocorrelation tax HC3 doesn't pay. The block-permutation
bootstrap (p_boot column in the headline table) remains the conservative
autocorrelation-aware test. HAC (Newey-West) over date-ordered
observations or cluster-robust by month would be the principled fix
for HC3 itself; deferred to separate PR cycle.

Note: 'beta_post' is the length+source+profession adjusted mean
shift in raw polarity units, not Hedges' g. Convert by dividing by
the residual SD if you want a standardized effect size.
