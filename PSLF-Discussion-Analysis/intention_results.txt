PER-AUTHOR LONGITUDINAL ANALYSIS
================================================================================
Round-7 floor-effect investigation follow-up.

DESIGN-CONSTRAINT FINDING: 7 of 8 events have <10 returning authors
with stance-classifiable posts in BOTH pre and post windows. The
PSLF discussant pool is dominated by single-window posters; within-
person stance dynamics are NOT IDENTIFIABLE FROM THIS CORPUS for most
events. Pooled pre/post stance shifts must therefore be interpreted as
DISCUSSANT-POOL COMPOSITION SHIFTS, not as borrower stance changes.
This is a fundamental limitation of forum-based discourse data, not
a fixable analysis choice.

Method: returning author = posted ≥1 stance-classifiable post in BOTH
windows. MODAL stance per window per author (deterministic tie-break:
rejecting > considering > pursuing > completed). McNemar's exact test
on matched-pairs rejecting transitions (paired data, not independent
samples). 95% CI via paired-bootstrap (B=2000). Authors filtered to
exclude [deleted], [removed], AutoModerator, bot accounts.

INTERPRETATION (Round-7 audit-revised):
  - The within-person Δ rejecting-rate is computed on a SELECTED
    subset (returning authors), who are by construction the most
    engaged posters. Engagement correlates with both committed-pursuing
    and frustrated-rejecting stances; the within-person estimate may
    not generalize to the full discussant pool.
  - For events meeting the n>=10 threshold, the 95% CI on the within-
    person Δ is wide enough at these sample sizes that the within-
    person estimate is statistically indistinguishable from both 0
    and from the pooled Δ. Treat as descriptive note only.
  - McNemar p tests H0: equal probability of rejecting↑ vs rejecting↓
    transitions among returning authors.
  - For 7 of 8 events, the data design itself does not support within-
    person inference. Composition-vs-change decomposition is
    NOT IDENTIFIED from this corpus alone.
  - See intention_within_vs_between_decomposition.png for the visual
    side-by-side comparison.
