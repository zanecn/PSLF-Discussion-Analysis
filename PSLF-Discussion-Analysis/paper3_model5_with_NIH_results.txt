NIH MASS-POINT DISCLOSURE (Round 17++ audit, IMPORTANT):
  Of 721 institutions in the analytical sample, only 92 (12.8%) have positive
  NIH FY2023 awards in the state-filtered NIH RePORTER match. The other 629
  institutions (87.2%) are coded as $0 NIH funding, and
  `log_nih_funding = log(1+0) = 0` is used as the regressor value for all 629.
  This produces a heavy mass-point at log_nih_funding=0 for ~87% of the
  institution-level sample, which biases the linear log_nih_funding coefficient
  toward 0 and makes the linear specification mis-specified. The reported
  coefficient (β=+0.02 pp/log, NS) is therefore not a clean dose-response
  estimate. A two-part specification (binary NIH-present + continuous-on-
  positive-only) or inverse-hyperbolic-sine transform would handle the zeros
  without the mass-point problem; both are recommended for the published
  version. **The PSLF-hostile coefficient (β=−18.07 pp, the headline) is robust
  to this NIH mis-specification** because removing log_nih_funding entirely
  (Model 4 → Model 5 step) only shifts PSLF-hostile β by < 0.2 pp.
