==========================================================================================
PAPER 3 TREND REGRESSION RESULTS (saved 2026-05-10, Round 17)
==========================================================================================

This script reproduces the multi-year trend numbers cited in:
  - PAPER_3_DRAFT_READY.md §3.3
  - PAPER_3_LOCKED_RESULTS_FINAL.md
  - MASTER_LOCKED_NUMBERS.md Paper 3 section

DESIGN:
  - Dataset: nrmp_program_level_2021_2026.csv (combined; March 2026 NRMP release added)
  - Total rows: 37,450 program-year observations
  - Filter: PSLF-hostile programs only (n=934)
  - Outcome: fill_rate * 100 (percentage points)
  - Model: fill_pp ~ year_centered + is_2026 + state FE + specialty FE
  - Cluster-robust SE on institution
  - year_centered = year - 2021 (so 2021=0, 2026=5)
  - is_2026 = binary indicator (1 if year==2026, 0 otherwise)

Interpretation:
  - year_centered coefficient = annual linear-trend slope on PSLF-hostile fill rate
  - is_2026 coefficient = additional 2026-specific deviation BEYOND the linear trend
    A non-significant is_2026 indicates 2026 narrowing is consistent with continuation
    of the pre-existing trend, NOT a Trump-EO-specific discontinuity.

==========================================================================================
RESULTS — PSLF-HOSTILE PROGRAMS, R17++ AGENT 2 M4 SENSITIVITY (is_post_eo = year>=2025)
==========================================================================================
  Rationale: 2025 NRMP ROL deadline was Feb 24, 2025 (BEFORE EO Mar 7);
  but applicants could have UPDATED preferences post-Match during SOAP
  (which only affects unfilled positions). The is_2026 binary spec assumes
  ZERO anticipation effect; the is_post_eo (year>=2025) binary catches
  any 2025-cycle anticipation effect (signaling/news-cycle anticipation).

Coefficient                     β (pp)         SE               95% CI      p-value
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  year_centered                +1.827       1.155         [-0.44, +4.09]         0.1138
  is_post_eo                   +0.538       3.480         [-6.28, +7.36]         0.8770

  COMPARISON OF SPECS:
    is_2026 (year==2026 only):    β=+2.98 pp, p=0.461
    is_post_eo (year>=2025):      β=+0.54 pp, p=0.877
    year_centered (with is_post_eo control): β=+1.827 pp/year, p=0.114

  INTERPRETATION:
    If is_post_eo IS significant but is_2026 is not, that would suggest a 2025
    anticipation effect that the is_2026 spec missed. If both are NS, then
    either the EO has no detectable causal impact OR both specs are underpowered.

INTERPRETATION:
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  PSLF-hostile programs:
    Linear trend (year_centered): +1.595 pp/year (p=0.1053)
    2026 deviation beyond trend (is_2026): +2.984 pp (p=0.4615)
    The 2026 narrowing is statistically indistinguishable from continuation of the
    pre-existing trend (is_2026 NS at α=0.05). This refutes the Trump-EO-specific
    discontinuity claim.

  PSLF-friendly programs (control):
    Year trend: +0.019 pp/year (p=0.8450)
    2026 deviation: -0.452 pp (p=0.2711)
    PSLF-friendly programs show a different (smaller, possibly negative) trajectory.
