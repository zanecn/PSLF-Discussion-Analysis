INTERPRETATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PSLF-hostile coefficient trajectory across model specifications:
  Model 1 (PSLF only): hostile = -18.76pp (p=1.094e-84)
  Model 2 (+ city quality): hostile = -17.90pp (p=1.018e-76)
  Model 3 (+ state FE): hostile = -19.00pp (p=7.999e-74)
  Model 4 (+ specialty FE): hostile = -18.56pp (p=3.628e-76)

Shrinkage from M1 to M4: -0.20pp

PSLF-hostile coefficient SURVIVES city quality + state FE + specialty FE.
This SUPPORTS the B5 substantive finding that PSLF eligibility
matters for residency competition even after controlling for the
structural factors that travel with academic-vs-for-profit hospital types.

POLICY USES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Resolves the P8 32% match-rate concern by using city-level aggregation
- Provides cleaner test of PSLF-mechanism vs quality-confound
- City-aggregate quality control is conservative (some NRMP institutions
  in a city may not host residencies at the lowest-quality CMS hospital)
- For paper: report Model 1 (raw gap) AND Model 4 (full controls) for
  honest interpretation of the PSLF-mechanism contribution
