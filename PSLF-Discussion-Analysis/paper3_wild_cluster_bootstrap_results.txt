INTERPRETATION:
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Cameron-Miller (2015) recommendation for n_clusters < 30 is to confirm
cluster-robust asymptotic p-values with wild-cluster bootstrap. If wild-cluster
p-values agree with the asymptotic p-values from cluster-robust SE, the
inference is robust to the small-cluster concern.

For Paper 3:
  - S1 has 23 hostile clusters → small-sample correction matters
  - S2/S3 have 9 hostile clusters → small-sample correction matters MORE

If wild-cluster bootstrap p-values are similar to cluster-robust p-values,
the headline survives the small-cluster concern. If wild-cluster p-values are
substantially larger, the cluster-robust SE was overstating precision.
