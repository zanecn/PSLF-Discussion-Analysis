*** ROUND 17 AUDIT REFRAME (2026-05-10) ***

This file's previous header described a "quasi-experimental DiD-style test of the
Trump EO effect." That framing has been RETRACTED. Reasons:

  (1) The 2026 narrowing of the PSLF-hostile differential continues a multi-year
      pre-existing trend, not an EO discontinuity. See paper3_trend_regression_results.txt:
        - Linear trend: year_centered = +1.60 pp/year (p=0.105)
        - is_2026 indicator BEYOND the linear trend = +2.98 pp (p=0.462, NS)
      The Δ β interpretation below is mechanically correct as a year-2026 vs years-2021-2025
      comparison, but it does NOT identify a causal EO effect.

  (2) ROL deadline issue: 2025 NRMP rank-order lists were locked Feb 24, 2025, BEFORE
      the EO was signed Mar 7, 2025. So 2025 ROLs are pre-EO. But the 2024-2025 trend
      already shows hostile fill rates rising (multi-year trend), so the 2026 jump is
      consistent with that trajectory rather than a discontinuous post-EO shift.

  (3) 2026-only n_hostile ≈ 171 program-year observations from ≤23 institutions.
      Power to distinguish a small EO effect from continuation of trend is limited.

INTERPRET AS: descriptive 6-year extension showing that the PSLF-hostile fill-rate
differential continues to narrow under the same trend observed 2021-2025. Causal
attribution to the Trump EO is NOT supported by this design.
