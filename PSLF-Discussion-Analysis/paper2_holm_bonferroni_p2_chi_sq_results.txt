Paper 2 §5.4 per-event 5-stance × 2-window chi-sq family.

Per Agent 5 M5 audit finding: §5.4 reports raw chi-sq p-values without
family-wise multiple-comparison correction. With 8 events in the family,
Holm-Bonferroni at α=0.05 sets per-test thresholds α/(m-rank+1) = 0.00625,
0.00714, ..., 0.05.

INTERPRETATION:
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Same conclusion as raw-p comparison: 6 of 8 events show significant pre/post
stance-distribution shifts, 2 of 8 do not (Biden v. Nebraska SCOTUS and
Payments Restart). The Holm-Bonferroni correction does NOT demote any event
that was significant at raw α=0.05; all 6 raw-significant events remain
significant after family-wise adjustment.

This confirms the R17 correction to Paper 2 §5.4 (which had previously claimed
'all 8 events p<10⁻⁴' — actually 6 of 8 significant at raw or Holm-Bonferroni
α=0.05; Biden v. Nebraska SCOTUS and Payments Restart are NS at either level).

Note: this addresses ONLY the 5-stance distribution chi-sq family (8 events).
The per-event × per-cohort × per-topic family (intention_results.txt:404+) is
larger and was not corrected here; that family-wise correction is recommended
if the per-event × per-cohort topic table is presented in the main paper.
