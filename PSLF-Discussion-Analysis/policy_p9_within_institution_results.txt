INTERPRETATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Within-institution analysis controls for time-invariant quality,
prestige, location, and reputation. If PSLF salience increase (Limited
Waiver) had a treatment effect on physician choice, we should see
differential pre/post fill-rate trajectories by PSLF class.

If DiD ≈ 0 for friendly vs hostile, the +12.86pp B5 cross-sectional
gap is structural (each institution's fill rate is stable; gap is
between-institution composition). PSLF salience did not differentially
affect institutions of different PSLF classes.
