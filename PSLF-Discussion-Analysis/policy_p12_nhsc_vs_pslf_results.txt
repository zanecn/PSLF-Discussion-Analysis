INTERPRETATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Two complementary federal forgiveness programs:
- NHSC: TARGETED (HPSA-specific, primary care-focused, requires service obligation)
- PSLF: BROAD-BASED (any 501(c)(3) employer, any specialty, no geographic
  targeting beyond employer eligibility)

If NHSC works as designed, we should see:
- Strong positive HPSA-NHSC correlation -> targeted allocation works
- Higher NHSC presence -> some compensating effect on workforce shortages

PSLF gap is LARGER in high-NHSC states (+37.44pp) than low
(+33.81pp). NHSC and PSLF may be ADDITIVE rather than
substitutive — both programs reinforce 501(c)(3) advantage.

POLICY USES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Congressional reform: shows TWO federal forgiveness programs operating
  with overlapping but distinct designs (NHSC=targeted, PSLF=broad)
- HRSA: NHSC is well-allocated to high-HPSA states (positive correlation)
- DOE/HRSA coordination: assess whether redundancy or complementarity
  with current PSLF + NHSC overlap
- For NHSC scaling: shows the program is doing what it should do; expansion
  could supplement workforce deficit areas where PSLF doesn't reach
