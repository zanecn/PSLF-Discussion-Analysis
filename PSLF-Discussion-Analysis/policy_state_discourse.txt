CAVEATS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- State mentions are noisy; many posts mention multiple states
- Reddit/SDN demographics skew young/educated/white (not representative
  of full PSLF-eligible borrower population)
- TextBlob polarity is one of three sentiment instruments; results would
  shift if using VADER or Claude
