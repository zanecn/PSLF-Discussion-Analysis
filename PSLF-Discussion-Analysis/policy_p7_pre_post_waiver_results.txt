INTERPRETATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Gap WIDENED after Waiver: +3.66 -> +12.97 (Δ +9.31pp)
PSLF salience increase drove additional differentiation.
This is closer to a treatment-effect interpretation.

POLICY USES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- For Congress: B5 finding now contextualized — gap is structural,
  not a treatment effect of Limited Waiver. PSLF program elimination
  would presumably erode this 12-13pp recruitment differential.
- For HCA / for-profit chains: data shows persistent recruitment
  disadvantage that pre-dates 2021 PSLF events.
- For physician workforce policy: the structural pattern means PSLF
  reform debates should focus on the ~13pp differential and which
  hospital types absorb (or compensate for) any reduction.
