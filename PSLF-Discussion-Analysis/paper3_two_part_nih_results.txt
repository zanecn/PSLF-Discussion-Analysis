PAPER 3 TWO-PART NIH SPECIFICATION (R17++ Agent 2 M2 fix)
==========================================================================================

Rationale: state-filtered NIH match identifies 92 of 721 institutions (12.8%) with
positive NIH funding; the other 629 (87.2%) are coded $0. Under log(1+0)=0 this
produces a mass-point at regressor=0 for 87% of the sample, biasing the linear
log_nih_funding coefficient toward zero.

Two-part decomposition:
  Part 1 (extensive): fill_rate ~ pslf_class + ... + nih_present + ...
  Part 2 (intensive): fill_rate ~ pslf_class + ... + log_nih_funding (positive-only)
