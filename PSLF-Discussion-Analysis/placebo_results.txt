DESIGN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Tests whether per-event sentiment shifts in PSLF posts could be a
general-community-mood effect rather than a PSLF-specific effect.

Comparators (placebos):
  * topical_near (n=7,750): OFF-PSLF posts in the SAME 21 subreddits
    over the same period. The strongest placebo design: same audience,
    same posting norms, just non-PSLF content.
  * askreddit    (n=177): general r/AskReddit posts. Limited
    coverage (most posts are 2026); useful only for late-period events.

VERDICT KEY
  ADEQUATE_NULL  - placebo |g| < 0.1: PSLF effect is community-specific
  MAGNITUDE_OK   - placebo |g| < 50% of PSLF |g|: mostly PSLF-specific
  CONFOUNDED     - placebo |g| >= 50% of PSLF |g|, same direction:
                   general community mood shift, not PSLF-specific
  OPPOSITE       - placebo direction opposite of PSLF: PSLF runs against mood
  UNDERPOWERED   - placebo n_pre or n_post < 20: cannot adjudicate
  ORIGINAL_NA    - PSLF effect itself was unestimable
